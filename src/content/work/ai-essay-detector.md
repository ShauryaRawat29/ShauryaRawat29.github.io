Built for the 2026 i12 HR Drive Hackathon. Flags machine-like writing using sentence-level statistical signals — perplexity, entropy, token probability — scored as z-scores against 6K-essay human baselines. No binary "AI or not" verdicts: per-sentence evidence tables instead.

Full-stack app with 200+ tests, ruff + mypy clean.
